Última actualización: 11 de octubre de 2026

Privacy policy — Derivatives & Integrals Formulas

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The app “Fórmulas Derivadas e Integrales” (package af27industries.derivadasintegrales) is published by 27industries SAS (Colombia).

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on servers, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or third-party services, and no internet permission.

Data kept on your phone

Your favorite formulas and your best practice streak are stored only on your device. They never leave it and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

The app is a study tool suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever adds features that use data, we will update this page and the Google Play Data safety section before releasing them.
